# The people in the end-to-end suite

The suite tells one story: a business owner moves his three companies onto Janus, and his people run them on it together. Each person signs in with their own login and browser, and they work on each other's records.

## Vikram Malhotra, Owner of the Malhotra group

- **Owns:** Malhotra Textiles (textile manufacturing, where most of the story happens), Malhotra Foods (food distribution) and Malhotra Tech Services (IT services).
- **Wants:** one place to see all three companies, without opening each one. He wants to know what needs him: approvals, overdue money, targets, filings. He runs routine work through several workflows, and he decides who may see what.
- **Worries about:** one company's data reaching another company's staff, and numbers he can't trust.
- **In the suite he:** signs up from the website, sets up all three companies, sets budgets, approves purchases and workflow runs, files workflows in folders, shares the Portfolio with his CFO, and checks the group figures.

## Shalini Rao, Head of HR (enterprise level)

- **Role in Janus:** Admin. It is the only role, short of Owner, that can manage all of HR.
- **Wants:** a correct organisation chart and employee records, clear approval routes, nobody seeing pay they shouldn't, and joiners and leavers handled by checklist. She shouldn't have to grant people access one at a time.
- **In the suite she:** joins by email invite, creates departments, invites fifteen employees, adds their records with reporting lines and pay, onboards a new joiner, opens an exit, gives final approval on leave, posts official announcements, kudos, polls and events.

## Anita Bose, Group CFO

- **Role in Janus:** Group finance, shared by Vikram across all three companies.
- **Wants:** to read the group's figures without being able to change any company.

## Sameer Kulkarni, General Manager at Malhotra Foods

- **Role in Janus:** Manager, in Malhotra Foods only.
- **In the suite:** proves that staff of one company can't see another company's records.

## How they work with each other

| Journey | Who passes work to whom |
|---|---|
| 01 Owner starts three companies | Vikram alone: signup, setup, adding companies, switching |
| 02 HR builds the company | Vikram invites Shalini; Shalini invites fifteen people and builds records; privacy of pay |
| 03 People work with HR | Priya asks for leave, Arjun approves, Shalini approves; Deepak's exit; announcements reach everyone |
| 04 Lead to cash | Priya captures a lead, Arjun makes it a won deal, Karan receives stock and dispatches, Finance invoices and takes payment |
| 05 Procure to pay | Karan raises a requisition, Vikram approves, Karan orders, Finance records the vendor's bill |
| 06 Projects | Neha starts a project from a Finance budget, plans tasks and a billable milestone, logs hours |
| 07 Support and IT | Arjun opens, assigns and resolves a ticket; feedback and renewal; assets, helpdesk, an IT change needing another person's approval |
| 08 Owner runs workflows | Vikram's workflows in two companies; a lead from Priya starts one; a ticket's run waits for his approval; the Runs tab |
| 09 People talk | Arjun and Priya chat live in two browsers; a cross-department channel with a thread and reaction; kudos, a poll people vote in, an event |
| 10 Owner monitors the group | Portfolio across three companies; sharing with the CFO; staff kept out of the Portfolio and of other companies |
| 11 Access by department | Whether each department can actually reach its own workspace |
